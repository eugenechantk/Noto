### 2026-08-24 — Root cause of the "app keeps going home": idb HID events sometimes route to SpringBoard

**What happened:** Throughout the capture-card work, apps "randomly" ended up on the home screen or under Spotlight. The real causes, established one by one: (1) idb HID gestures/keys are sometimes handled by SpringBoard instead of the foreground app on iOS 26 — a mid-screen swipe-down opened Spotlight, multi-line `idb ui text` (Return presses) kicked the app out; (2) `launch` (flowdeck or idb) is a NO-OP foregrounding-wise when the app is already running in the background — screenshots then show the home screen and look like a crash; (3) direct plist edits to seed @AppStorage are invisible (cfprefsd caches) — use `defaults write` via the seed script.
**What better looks like:** Foreground reliably by tapping the app icon (or terminate-then-launch); never multi-line-type via idb (seed drafts through `seed-vault.sh --draft-file` instead); when a screenshot shows home, first check `pgrep` — the app is usually alive in the background, not crashed.

**What better looks like:** Chrome that depends on text-view state (placeholders, empty states, counters) belongs inside the text view, updated in the delegate callback — never derived from a debounced SwiftUI mirror.
